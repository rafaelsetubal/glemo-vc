DILIGENCE • LEGAL / COMPLIANCE
LEGAL / COMPLIANCE SUMMARY

Legal / Compliance Summary

Asset-class-neutral liquidity and distribution rail for tokenized real-world assets.

The legal posture should protect the public marketplace front and neutral-rail thesis while avoiding issuance, yield, advisory, unrestricted securities distribution or guaranteed-liquidity claims.

Operating Perimeter

GlemO should describe its role as coordination, workflow, discovery, reporting, buyer activation and distribution-readiness infrastructure. It should not claim to issue, tokenize, custody, underwrite, advise on or distribute regulated assets unless a specific structure is approved by qualified counsel and implemented with regulated providers.

Public Front Controls

  • Public pages should show high-level opportunity discovery and market education, not unrestricted securities offers.
  • Detailed access, documents, pricing, allocation and execution workflows should be gated by jurisdiction, investor status, KYC/KYB and provider rules.
  • Marketing language should avoid yield, guaranteed liquidity, price support, asset cash-flow entitlement or universal access.
  • Each opportunity should have a provider, jurisdiction, instrument type, restriction legend and access-status workflow before being shown beyond discovery.

Token Controls

  • The token is a supporting incentive layer for dApps, AI agents, developer tooling and verified contribution.
  • Token holders should not be described as receiving RWA revenue, asset cash flow, equity value or investor distributions.
  • Any material change to token objective requires unanimous written approval from the project equity partners and investor consent parties defined in definitive documents.
  • No token launch, float release, market-maker deployment or public token campaign should occur before legal, security, wallet and market-conduct gates.

GlemO is a neutral liquidity and distribution infrastructure layer. The company should coordinate discovery, readiness, analytics, participant routing, reporting and controlled workflow. It should not present itself as issuer, custodian, exchange, underwriter, market maker or investment adviser unless a specific counsel-approved structure is implemented.

CORPORATE STRUCTURE

Corporate Structure

Asset-class-neutral liquidity and distribution rail for tokenized real-world assets.

Investors should see plainly which entity is raising, what it owns and how the existing GlemO operating history connects to it.

Status: the structure below is proposed and subject to counsel. It is not yet implemented. Entity names, jurisdictions and licence terms are indicative and will be confirmed in definitive documents before closing.

Why Two Offshore Entities

Market practice separates protocol stewardship from commercial operation. A token issued by a neutral steward is assessed differently from a token issued by a profit-seeking operating company, and exchanges, counsel and institutional investors increasingly expect that separation to be visible in the structure rather than asserted in a memo.

Entity Map

EntityRoleWhat it holds
GlemO S/A (Brazil)Existing operating companyCurrent Web2 real estate sales and AI distribution operations, brand, existing customer and partner relationships. Source of the licensed assets.
GlemO LLC (US)Existing US operating entityUS-facing operations and contracts as currently established.
Cayman entityToken and protocol stewardToken issuance, protocol IP, treasury wallets and governance. Deliberately separated from commercial operation.
BVI Business CompanyOperating arm of the railPlatform build and operation, originator and provider agreements, buyer activation workflows, commercial revenue.

Which Entity Raises

The pre-seed round is raised into the operating entity. The instrument is a SAFE plus a token warrant, so equity economics sit with the operating company while token rights are satisfied from the steward entity under governance rules. Investors should confirm this split in definitive documents rather than infer it from this page.

Licence From The Existing Group

The operating entity does not begin as an empty shell. The existing GlemO group grants it a licence over the assets the platform depends on. For that licence to support valuation, its terms matter more than its price.

TermIntended positionWhy investors ask
ScopeBrand, platform code, operating data and the AI matching and activation engine.A brand-only licence does not support a platform valuation.
DurationPerpetual.A term licence creates a cliff the investor cannot control.
RevocabilityIrrevocable.Founder control on both sides is the reason this must be documented, not assumed.
ExclusivityExclusive within the tokenized-asset distribution field.Prevents the licensor competing with the funded entity.
Change of controlSurvives a sale or restructuring of the licensor.Protects the asset if the Brazilian group changes hands.
ConsiderationNominal consideration rather than free, documented at arm's length.Related-party transfers at zero value attract tax and transfer-pricing scrutiny.

How Operating History Transfers

The execution metrics cited in the deck belong to the existing Brazilian operation, not to the newly formed entity. That is normal for a group structure, and it should be stated rather than blurred. What transfers is the operating engine: buyer-to-asset matching, multi-channel activation workflows and cross-border operating experience. What does not transfer is the customer base, because the buyer profile and regulatory perimeter are different.

Investor read: treat the existing operation as evidence that the team can run distribution workflows, not as revenue of the entity being funded. Evidence status for those metrics is tracked in the Evidence Status page.

Competitive Positioning

Asset-class-neutral liquidity and distribution rail for tokenized real-world assets.

GlemO should position against the real competitive set without pretending incumbents do not matter.

Most RWA players are optimized for one side of the market: issuance, tokenization technology, asset management, custody, trading venue or their own marketplace. GlemO's intended position is different: a neutral coordination rail that can source opportunities, activate buyers and route market participants without being tied to one asset book, one chain, one bank or one venue.
