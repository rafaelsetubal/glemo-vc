RAIL CONTINUITY PLAN

Rail Continuity Plan

Asset-class-neutral liquidity and distribution rail for tokenized real-world assets.

GlemO must be credible even if a specific bank, real estate company, tokenizer, custodian, venue or market maker does not proceed.

Continuity Principle

The company should never depend on one institution as the unlock. A neutral rail is valuable because it can route across multiple participants and preserve optionality.

FunctionPrimary pathFallback path
Asset supplyReal estate credit originators, private-credit platforms, receivables owners, infrastructure sponsors, asset managers and tokenized-asset originators.Brokers, operators, asset managers, tokenization platforms and direct mandates.
DistributionBanks, fintechs, brokers and communities.Qualified buyer CRM, wealth channels, fund networks and international investor targets.
Regulated railsCounsel-approved providers for KYC, custody, trustee/FIDC and reporting.Multiple provider shortlist by jurisdiction and function.
Liquidity readinessMM/venue conversations after gates.Buyer-response data, redemption/execution pathways and transparent reporting before market-maker spend.
TechnologyDedicated platform team.Modular architecture, third-party APIs and staged build scope.

Investor Message

Banks are strong participants, but neutrality is the product. GlemO should be able to say which market function each participant provides and how the company continues if any single participant slows down.
