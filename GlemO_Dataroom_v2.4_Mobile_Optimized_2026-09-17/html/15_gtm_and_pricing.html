GTM & PRICING

GTM & Pricing

Asset-class-neutral liquidity and distribution rail for tokenized real-world assets.

The go-to-market motion should prove that GlemO can actively source supply and provoke demand, not wait for marketplace traffic.

Initial Wedge

The first wedge should be paid readiness and distribution workflow for tokenized real estate and adjacent RWA opportunities. The customer is the participant that needs credible access to qualified capital, better market presentation, provider routing or buyer-response evidence.
