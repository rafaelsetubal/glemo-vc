ENTERPRISE SAAS OFFER

Enterprise SaaS Offering

The first repeatable revenue wedge for sellers, originators and distribution partners.

This page converts the AI concierge and distribution analytics concept into a concrete product package that can be sold, measured and renewed.

Commercial Thesis

The enterprise SaaS package is designed for banks, originators, tokenizers and sellers that need better market intelligence, asset-distribution analytics and buyer-readiness support. It should not look like custom consulting. It should look like a recurring product with a clear monthly deliverable cycle.

Two-Plan Offer

PlanPriceIncludedProof metric
Free ListingUS$0Basic entity profile, asset discovery listing request, document checklist and limited readiness view.Number of qualified sellers and completed intake records.
Enterprise AI DistributionUS$990/month or annual equivalentAI reports, market x-ray, comparative benchmarks, transaction leakage visibility, official channel map, private scenario request and concierge queue.Paid accounts, report delivery, retention, expansion and seller-attributed routed flow.

Monthly Deliverables

  • One consolidated RWA market report for the seller or originator category.
  • One asset/channel performance view covering impressions, leads, conversion and external transaction leakage where available.
  • Benchmarking against comparable assets, channels, pricing, demand segments and disclosure quality.
  • Private scenario request processed through the GlemO AI prompt chain and reviewed before delivery.
  • Action list for next month: documentation gaps, channel tests, buyer segment focus and readiness improvements.

Investor Read

The SaaS layer is important because it lets GlemO monetize before large transaction volume exists. It also creates proprietary market data: sellers, channels, buyer signals, pricing references, disclosure quality and demand friction. That data improves the future liquidity and distribution layer.
