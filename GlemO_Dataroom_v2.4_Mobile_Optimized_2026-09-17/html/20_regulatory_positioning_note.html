REGULATORY POSITIONING

Regulatory Positioning Note

GlemO should be positioned as infrastructure before it is positioned as a transaction venue.

This note clarifies the intended regulatory posture for the pre-seed diligence stage. It is not legal advice and should be converted into counsel-approved operating policies before launch.

Core Position

GlemO is a neutral liquidity and distribution infrastructure layer. The company should coordinate discovery, readiness, analytics, participant routing, reporting and controlled workflow. It should not present itself as issuer, custodian, exchange, underwriter, market maker or investment adviser unless a specific counsel-approved structure is implemented.

Counsel Workstream

  • Prepare a financial-promotion review for UK-facing materials, meetings and follow-up communications.
  • Prepare a US securities analysis for SAFE plus token warrant, token objective covenant and any future token distribution.
  • Define the launch jurisdictions where asset details, allocation and transaction features may be shown.
  • Approve public marketplace legends, risk warnings, cooling-off or friction steps where required, and user categorization controls.
  • Define whether each revenue line is SaaS, service fee, referral fee, transaction fee or regulated activity in each operating path.

Reference Sources

Current public regulatory posture supports this conservative framing. The UK FCA states that cryptoasset promotions to UK consumers are subject to the financial promotions regime and must use permitted routes. The SEC has also stated that tokenized securities remain securities where the underlying instrument is a security.
