Market Scale

Asset-class-neutral liquidity and distribution rail for tokenized real-world assets.

Market sizing should support the wedge without becoming a valuation promise.

Market Context

The RWA market is already large enough to justify a focused infrastructure wedge, but still immature enough that distribution, liquidity and standardization remain unsolved. GlemO should use market size as context, not as a revenue forecast.

Read this first: the figures below are illustrative market-sizing only. They are not revenue projections, liquidity promises, valuation support or securities offering language. They show why distribution and liquidity readiness are worth solving.

Operating Implication

SignalImplication for GlemO
Tokenized assets exist across multiple providers and networks.Issuance is not the only hard problem. Coordination and distribution are the next constraint.
Real estate tokenization still faces secondary-market and standardization bottlenecks.A neutral rail can be valuable if it improves discovery, reporting, buyer activation and liquidity readiness.
Public marketing of crypto/RWA opportunities is regulated in major jurisdictions.The marketplace front must separate discovery from restricted offer/access workflows.
AI is becoming a core operating advantage in asset management and distribution.GlemO should prove AI with workflow KPIs, not generic AI language.
