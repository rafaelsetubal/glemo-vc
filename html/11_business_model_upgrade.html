BUSINESS MODEL

Business Model

Asset-class-neutral liquidity and distribution rail for tokenized real-world assets.

The business model should be read as a staged operating rail, not a passive marketplace or token story.

Business Model Thesis

GlemO should become a coordination business with software economics over time. The first commercial wedge is not charging the public to browse assets. It is helping asset supply become investable, visible, routed, measured and ready for qualified demand through a repeatable operating workflow.

What Makes It Venture-Backable

  • The same workflow can serve many originators, assets, buyers and providers.
  • Data compounds: each opportunity, buyer response and provider status improves routing quality.
  • AI agents can reduce manual coordination cost in sourcing, diligence, matching and reporting.
  • The rail can monetize before speculative token or secondary-market outcomes.
  • Neutrality allows GlemO to work with asset issuers, banks, brokers, tokenizers and venues without becoming captive to one stack.

Cold Critique

The largest risk is becoming a bespoke advisory business with software slides. The countermeasure is to package the first 90-180 days around repeatable workflows, measurable buyer activation and a small number of standardized paid motions.
