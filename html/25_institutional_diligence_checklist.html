Institutional Diligence Checklist

Asset-class-neutral liquidity and distribution rail for tokenized real-world assets.

A maturity checklist for turning Token2049 conversations into credible follow-up with funds, strategic partners and regulated institutions.

Minimum Robust Package

AreaRequired EvidenceStatus For Meetings
NarrativeDeck, memo, canonical language, asset-class map and clear use/avoid language.Ready, with asset-class-neutral framing.

Critical Investor Questions To Be Ready For

  • Are you a marketplace, broker, issuer, exchange, adviser, market maker or infrastructure provider?
  • Which revenue lines are regulated activity versus SaaS, services, routing or reporting?
  • What can be shown publicly, what requires gated access and what requires NDA?
  • What is the first asset category and why does the product expand beyond it?
  • Who owns origination, custody, tokenization, registry, payments, compliance, market access and investor support?
  • What objective evidence justifies a seed round at a materially higher valuation?

Pre-Launch Gaps To Close

  • Final counsel memo for target launch jurisdictions and investor communication rules.
  • Provider shortlist with backup options for KYC/KYB, custody, tokenization, payments, reporting and legal vehicles.
  • Documented pilot criteria for the first two to three assets or asset programs.
  • Production access policy for data room, investor logs, file versioning and permissions.
  • Confirmed founder/team biographies, role allocation and external adviser boundaries.
