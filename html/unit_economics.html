UNIT ECONOMICS

Unit Economics

Asset-class-neutral liquidity and distribution rail for tokenized real-world assets.

Economics must be separated so investors do not confuse current service proof, future platform revenue and token incentives.

DriverUnitTarget evidence
Readiness sprintFee per originator, asset or programPaid scope, checklist delivered, conversion to platform workflow.
Workflow/reportingMonthly participant feeUsage, recurring invoice, retention.
Marketplace frontProfile, access, API or reporting feeListed opportunities, buyer response, qualified access requests.
Buyer activationCampaign or success/service fee where approvedCRM, meetings, conversion and counsel approval.
Provider routingIntegration or workflow fee where approvedProvider agreement and usage logs.
Coordination / routed flowBasis-point fee on approved routed volume, where contracted and legally permittedRouting agreement, counsel memo, settled volume records and fee invoices. Illustrative sizing only; see Market Scale.
Token ecosystemNo revenue lineAccepted dApps, AI-agent logs and contribution ledger.
Liquidity readinessPolicy-controlled spend and reportingMM policy, venue review, market data if deployed.

Gross Margin Logic

Early margins may look service-heavy. The key is whether repeatable templates, data, AI agents and provider workflows reduce manual hours over time. Investors should see improving delivery time per opportunity and lower cost per qualified buyer response.
