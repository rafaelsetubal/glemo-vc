PRODUCT MVP SCOPE

Product MVP Scope

Asset-class-neutral liquidity and distribution rail for tokenized real-world assets.

The MVP should be narrow enough to ship and strong enough to prove the rail.

90-Day MVP

ModuleWhat shipsWhat waits
Opportunity intakeAsset profile, owner/status fields, document checklist and readiness score.Complex valuation engine.
Marketplace frontControlled discovery pages for selected opportunities with restriction language.Open retail investing flow.
Buyer activation CRMQualified buyer segments, outreach status, response logging and next action.Automated capital allocation.
Provider matrixProvider roles, jurisdictions, constraints and status tracking.Deep integrations with every provider.
ReportingInvestor/participant reporting pack and data-room evidence export.Fully automated analytics product.
Security/authTracked investor access, permissions, no client-side-only sensitive access.Enterprise SSO unless lead investor requires it.

180-Day Proof

  • First live controlled marketplace profiles.
  • Repeatable opportunity intake process.
  • Buyer activation response data.
  • Provider matrix with active conversations and constraints.
  • Monthly cash/burn reporting against the US$2M plan.
  • Token objective covenant drafted but token launch still gated.

Localization Scope

The current investor interface supports English, Portuguese, Spanish, French and Arabic. Chinese is a phase-two localization target. Investor, legal and diligence materials remain English-governed until counsel-approved localized versions are released.

Product Boundary

The MVP should not try to be a full exchange, regulated issuer, custodian, universal tokenization engine or retail investment app. It should prove the coordination layer first.
