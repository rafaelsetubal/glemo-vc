WALLET / FLOAT POLICY

Wallet / Float Policy

Asset-class-neutral liquidity and distribution rail for tokenized real-world assets.

Token execution must be controlled, evidence-based and subordinate to platform delivery.

Policy Principle

GO should be managed as ecosystem infrastructure, not a financing shortcut. Releases should be tied to accepted platform contributions, dApp delivery, AI-agent workflows, developer tooling, verified campaigns and market operations approved by governance.

Non-Negotiables

  • No token claim on RWA cash flows.
  • No promised secondary liquidity.
  • No launch before token objective covenant, wallet policy, audit and disclosure controls.
  • No material objective change without unanimous written approval under definitive documents.
