Evidence Plan

Asset-class-neutral liquidity and distribution rail for tokenized real-world assets.

Every strong claim in the deck should map to evidence. This page defines the evidence architecture investors should expect.

Evidence Standard

A VC should never need to infer whether a claim is real. The strongest version of the data room has a one-to-one relationship between claim, file, status, owner and date.
