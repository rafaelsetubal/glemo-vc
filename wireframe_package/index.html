Expected Delivery

The platform is expected to organize tokenized real-world asset offerings in a commercial marketplace, supporting both pools and standalone assets with predefined quantity, unit value, eligibility, risk and liquidity rules.

Usability Intent

The experience is designed to let users browse first, understand each asset, compare risk and then enter the controlled investment gate through wallet connection, registration, KYC and investor profile.

Beta Scope

Each actor dashboard puts the most important numbers first, then charts, risk signals and action queues. V4.7 keeps the complete product vision visible while separating MVP1 build, manual controls and future expansion layers.
